Regional aerial imagery / 地區航照 / 地域航空写真

Reviewed 2026-11-21. This first version selects imagery in Taiwan, metropolitan France (including Corsica), and the conterminous United States. It checks actual pixels before capture and falls back to streets when unavailable. Availability, capture dates and resolution vary. Each saved movie includes a single viewport image plus source and license notices; it is not a regional tile-pack downloader.

  • Taiwan: NLSC PHOTO2 orthophotos, under NLSC service terms. Public video display with source credit; no bulk downloads. This service is not assumed to be CC BY merely because it is government-operated.
  • France: IGN BD ORTHO, Licence Ouverte 2.0; delivered via Géoplateforme WMTS. Images are cropped/reprojected and notices preserved. Commercial reuse requires attribution.
  • United States: USGS / USDA NAIP imagery, public-domain dataset. USGS credit policy. This applies to the selected NAIP dataset, not every photograph on a government website.
  • Country-selection masks: Natural Earth 1:50m, public domain. Used only for provider selection, not displayed boundaries; source and file checksum are retained in the selection catalogue.
