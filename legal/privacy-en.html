TrailMemory Privacy Policy

Updated 21 November 2026 · TrailMemory

Contact the TrailMemory development team at iliadhsiao@icloud.com. No account, advertising SDK, cross-app tracking or developer analytics service is included.

On-device data

Routes, photos, videos, music, avatars and health data are processed on your device. We have no server receiving this content. Sharing or exporting sends files to the app or recipient you select; movies may contain locations, photos and activity summaries. Preview before sharing. Journey and activity records that may contain health or fitness data are excluded from system backups. Other files follow your Apple backup settings. Keep your own copies of exports you wish to retain.

Maps and weather

Online maps request your selected map area from OpenFreeMap (including Cloudflare), Mapterhorn, or Taiwan NLSC. Providers receive your IP address, app user agent and tile coordinates, which can reveal the precise area viewed. We do not send your full route file or photos.

When weather is enabled, we check after the journey gets a location and about every 30 minutes thereafter, reusing cached forecasts until the provider permits an update. MET Norway receives coordinates rounded to two decimals (about 1 km) and your IP address; it stores access logs in Norway. The app user agent contains our support contact, not your account. Ending a trip or disabling weather stops scheduled downloads.

Your choices

Route recording needs location and can continue while the screen is locked after you start. Photo access can be selected, full or denied; manual import remains available. Motion & Fitness, Apple Health and notifications are optional. Camera and microphone are requested only for relevant capture features. Photos are not sent to AI services; external AI creation and import are your own actions.

Health

Only authorized health types within journey periods are read to display activity. Health data is not used for ads, marketing, sale or diagnosis, and we do not write to Apple Health. Device availability, permissions and sync delays can leave gaps.

Retention and deletion

Trips stay on-device until you swipe left on a home-screen trip and delete it. Deleting removes its app-owned media copies and movie, not originals in Photos. Uninstalling removes local app data; exported files, photo originals and backups must be managed separately. Revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Provider log retention follows their policies; we cannot delete their logs for you.

Support and third parties

If you email support, we receive your address and the content you choose to send, only to handle your request. Avoid sending unnecessary health or precise-location data. TestFlight testing and crash feedback are governed by Apple separately.

Offline map and terrain packs stay on-device and can be managed or deleted from Offline Maps on the home screen. Browsing imported packs needs no map connection; downloading or switching to an online map uses the network.

Optional Mapbox satellite and offline maps

This feature uses the native Mapbox mobile SDK for satellite imagery, streets and terrain. Online requests include IP address, viewed area, device and SDK usage information. The SDK may collect de-identified location and usage data by default; opt out through Mapbox Telemetry in the map attribution/information control. Photos, health samples and complete journey files are not provided to Mapbox. My location uses device location while the map is open.

Downloaded areas remain on-device. Offline only disconnects the Mapbox network stack; areas and details not downloaded may be blank. Deleted data is reclaimed according to SDK cache policy. This switch does not disable journey weather or other map providers; airplane mode stops ordinary network requests. These Mapbox maps are currently for in-app viewing and are not used in movie exports.

Automatic regional imagery (updated 21 November 2026)

For movie maps, the app selects aerial imagery on-device using the journey area: Taiwan NLSC, France IGN Géoplateforme (data.geopf.fr), or USGS National Map (imagery.nationalmap.gov). Where imagery is unavailable, it uses world street maps. Availability probes also use the network. Providers receive IP address, app user agent and tile or bounding-box coordinates, revealing the area viewed, but not photos, health data or the complete route file. Mapterhorn coverage requests identify original terrain sources for attribution. Saved images, terrain and notices remain with the journey for offline movie playback; this is not a whole-country or worldwide offline tile download.
